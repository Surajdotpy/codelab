# Code of Conduct

CodeLab is built for teaching, and children may use it. We want everyone who takes part,
whether by writing code, reporting bugs, or answering questions, to feel welcome and safe.

## Our standard

Please:
- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say how it could be better.
- Assume good intent, and ask if something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and appropriate for a project used in schools.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Sexual or violent content
- Sharing other people's private information, such as real names, emails, or photos of
  students, without permission
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing for a decision after the maintainers have given an answer

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break them,
and may block people who keep doing so. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other space where the project is represented.

## Reporting a problem

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainers through
GitHub (for example by messaging a maintainer through their profile, or using the
**Report content** option on the comment). Reports are handled privately where possible.
